1.1. Project Context 

This project aims to partially reproduce the Study developed in Paper 1 presented as a Project option and part of the Genome Analysis Course, “RNA-seq and Tn-seq reveal fitness determinants of vancomycin-resistant Enterococcus faecium during growth in human serum”. By doing so, as a student, I deepen my understanding of general genome analysis and used methods in a professional setting, with the support of the study, which is being mimicked, knowing clearly the expected results. 

Methods: 

1 – Genome Sequencing, Assembly and Bioinformatic analysis 

E. faecium E745 was sequenced discarded contigs with low coverage. 15 contigs (1 contig of 2.77 Mbp chromosome. Remaining contigs (in their case 5 circular plasmid sequences and 5 non-overlapping contigs). These 5 contigs were aligned against the NCBI Genbank database and all were found to be part of the E. faecium plasmid pMG1.
